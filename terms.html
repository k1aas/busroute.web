6. Privacy and data

Location access is required to use this app. It is requested when you enter your licence key. During guidance the app also asks to keep using location while the screen is locked, so guidance continues if the phone locks mid-run.

Recorded runs, stop positions, arrival times, student counts and notes are stored on your phone. The app contacts only our own server, osrm.busroute.app: to compute directions (the route's GPS path, not kept), to download map data (which map area is needed, not kept), and to activate a licence key (key, random device identifier, GPS position stored rounded to about 20 km, IP address, date and time). Your bus company can see the approximate location and date of each activation.

Our server is in Germany (Hetzner Online). Activation records are kept while the licence is active and deleted within 12 months after it ends; server logs, which may include IP addresses, are kept for up to 14 days. No advertising, analytics or tracking. No personally identifiable student information is collected.
