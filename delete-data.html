How to have your BusRoute data deleted

Data on your phone

Recorded runs, routes, notes and the saved map are stored only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall the BusRoute Driver app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Nothing else is needed.

Data on our server

When a licence key is activated, our server stores an activation record: the licence key, a random device identifier created by the app, the date and time, the internet (IP) address, and the location rounded to about 20 km.

To have the activation records for your phone deleted:

  • Email klaas@busroute.app with the subject "Delete my BusRoute data".
  • Say which licence key you used, and roughly when and where you activated it, so we can find your phone's records.

We delete the records and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: every activation record for your device — device identifier, date and time, IP address and approximate location.
  • Kept: the licence key itself, which belongs to your bus company, not to you. It contains no personal data.
  • Routine server logs, which may include IP addresses, are deleted automatically within 14 days.

Without asking

Activation records are deleted automatically within 12 months after the licence ends. See the Privacy Policy for details.
