About Us

A bit of history

Background

Klaas, the developer of this app, worked for a greater part of his careeer as an instructor for a Medical X-Ray company. During these years, he noticed multiple occasions where the Service Reps he trained didn't have the proper tools for equipment adjustment and image quality measurement. This led to the development of software tools that utilized Excel macros and C++ that were used extensively and worldwide.

How does this align with School Bus Driving

After retiring and taking on a job as a school bus driver in 2019, he noticed a glaring deficiency in this business in the way substitute bus driving was handled. Just as X-Ray Service Reps, bus drivers did not have the proper tools to do the job. This led to a search for a way to make substitute bus driving safer and easier.

Looking for a Solution

After driving for 7 years, he was able to function as a Subject Matter Expert and define the needs, indicate how the User Interface should be. The recent developments in AI made it possible to generate a basic working app that was extensively tested and streamlined with the help of expo.dev and github.com. After many hundreds of builds, an app emerged that was placed in both the Play Store and the App Store. His previous experience made it possible to function as QA, just as in his former career life.
