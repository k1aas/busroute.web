About Us

A bit of history

Background

Klaas, the developer of this app, worked for a greater part of his careeer as an instructor for a large Medical X-Ray company. During these years, he came across multiple occasions where the Service Reps he trained did not have the proper tools for equipment adjustment and image quality measurement. This led to the development of software tools that utilized Excel macros and C++. These tools ended up being used extensively and worldwide.

How does this align with School Bus Driving?

After retiring and taking on a job as a school bus driver in 2019, he noticed a glaring deficiency in the way substitute bus driving was handled. Just as X-Ray Service Reps, bus drivers did not have the proper tools to do the job.

This led to a search for a way to make substitute bus driving safer and less stressful. There are expensive applications on the market that also solve some problems. However, they place a huge burden on the branch offices and transportation services. The quest was on to develop an inexpensive tool that did not burden anyone, was easy to use in the school bus industry as it functions in North America, Australia and New Zealand.

Looking for a Solution

After driving school bus for 7 years, Klaas had sufficient experience to be able to build a tool. This meant functioning as Subject Matter Expert, indicating how the User Interface should be, and also testing Quality Assurance.

The recent developments in AI made it possible to generate a functional JavaScript app of 10k lines of code that was extensively tested and streamlined within expo.dev and github.com. After many months, hundreds of builds and a huge amount of test drives, the app is now available for both Android and iPhone.

What about the finances?

In order to make this app usable for large and small school bus entities, the price is kept at a reasonable level. The transport company purchases an access key that is then available for all drivers. Renewal occurs yearly for the price of a cup of coffee per driver.
